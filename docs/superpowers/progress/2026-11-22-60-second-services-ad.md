# Liberty Digital: 60-second services advert render
Date: 2026-11-22
Source: Approved conversational sales advert for Nigerians living in Italy.

## Production goals
- 60-second, vertical social advert using checked-in Liberty Digital office and Nigerian service imagery.
- Explain passport, NIN, BVN, affidavits/legalisation and eVisa/certificates independently and accurately.
- Free locally synthesised Kokoro narration, original gentle music, legible subtitles and a WhatsApp close.
- Avoid simulated endorsements, fabricated customer claims, promises of guaranteed government outcomes or fake office footage.
- Use existing Remotion renderer and media allowlist with a deterministic standalone offline render.
- No Vercel deployment, no website code changes, no automatic public social posting.

## Implementation
- Storyboard: `video-worker/campaigns/2026-11-23-services-ad.json`.
- Rendering: `video-worker/scripts/render-weekly-ad.mjs`.
- CI job: `.github/workflows/render-liberty-weekly-ad.yml`.
- MP4 generated by GitHub Actions and retained as downloadable artifact for 30 days.
- Asset use is for requested owner review. Confirm rights before publication.

## Validation
The renderer checks scene timings, audible narration, MP4 audio+video streams and approximately 60 seconds duration.
