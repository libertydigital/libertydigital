# Liberty Digital: design system and conversion surfaces

Maintained 2026-10-30.

## Product
Liberty Digital Consulting offers independent Nigerian documentation preparation and consulting support in Rome and across Italy. It is not an embassy, bank, NIS, NIMC, NPC or official issuing body. The public visitor's main job is to choose a support service and send a short enquiry.

## Visual identity
- Palette: forest #10211c and #0e3527; paper #fffaf3 and #fbf8f2; champagne #ead7a4; gold #b18a51; navy #112031 and navy-soft #3f5266.
- Display face: Cormorant Garamond through font-serif; body face: Manrope through font-sans.
- Layout: container-premium/container-shell; generous editorial whitespace; existing document-themed imagery. Avoid fabricated badges, reviews, authorisations, business accreditations or stock-office claims.
- Homepage signature: the original rotating Passport/NIN/BVN/eVisa editorial hero, followed by the nine-service image carousel with full-bleed photography. Preserve the existing section layout and service card design unless a redesign is explicitly requested.
- Public marketing pages: persuade/read. Protected lead admin: operate. Reuse existing UI primitives.

## UX contract
- Initial public enquiry needs only contact details and a short description; sensitive files and credentials are not requested until a qualified later stage.
- Pricing uses the current SERVICES.price catalogue. Positive numeric prices are indicative Liberty service charges; zero means quote required, not free. Official authority charges are separate. Validate with the business before production.
- Mobile service pages have one sticky action pair: Request support and WhatsApp. Suppress global floating WhatsApp on those service paths to prevent overlap.
- Analytics must remain consent-gated, with events limited to service slug, route and placement, never personal data.
- Confirmed paid revenue is a protected admin-only field in existing lead JSON in integer EUR cents. The admin screen does not process payments. Count only money manually recorded as received.
- Interactive controls need keyboard focus, readable contrast, usable 44px+ targets and reduced-motion support. Essential content works without JS animation.
- Internal linking should connect useful related services and contextual resource guides without duplicate articles.

## Verified-facts backlog
Google Business Profile ownership, real opening hours, authentic customer reviews, official accreditation and business-confirmed prices require firsthand confirmation. Never fabricate them.
